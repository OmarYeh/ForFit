# ForFit - E-Commerce Fashion Store

ForFit is a next-generation e-commerce fashion platform that revolutionizes online shopping by integrating Augmented Reality (AR) virtual try-on and an AI-powered fashion assistant. This cutting-edge application allows users to visualize clothing on their bodies in real time through pose tracking technology and receive intelligent outfit recommendations from FitBot.

## About ForFit
ForFit is designed to bridge the gap between online shopping and real-world fashion experience. It leverages AR and AI to enhance the customer journey, providing a highly interactive and personalized shopping experience. By allowing users to virtually try on outfits, ForFit eliminates uncertainty, increases confidence in purchase decisions, and reduces return rates.

## Features

### E-Commerce Platform
- A seamless browsing and shopping experience for fashion lovers.
- Secure and intuitive checkout process with multiple payment options.
- Wishlist, cart management, and order tracking functionalities.

### AR-Powered Virtual Try-On
- Built with **Vuforia SDK, Unity, and Google MediaPipe** for a high-precision experience.
- Real-time pose tracking that enables users to see outfits on their bodies.
- Supports various types of clothing, ensuring an immersive shopping experience.

### FitBot - AI Fashion Assistant
- AI chatbot powered by **GPT-4o** to help pick outfits.
- Personalized recommendations based on user preferences.
- Fashion tips and trend analysis.
